What is Dick Theft Auto?

Somewhere between a stolen lowrider and a sunset that should probably be illegal, a small pink legend slid on his shades and said: “Let’s ride.” That’s $DTA.

No cutscene you can’t skip. No tutorial that takes an hour. Just neon, palm trees, memes at highway speed and one mascot who has never once paid for parking.

Think of it as a meme-powered road trip down a very pink coastline. The cops are confused. The radio is too loud. Respect is the only stat that matters.
